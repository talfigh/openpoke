I'm sorry to hear about the burst pipe and the water in your basement. Here are the general steps to start a claim:

1. Contact your insurance company as soon as possible to report the water damage. Prompt reporting helps avoid problems with the claim.
2. Keep your photos of the damage, with dates if you can, and add a short video if possible.
3. Keep the plumber's invoice and the repair report, which should describe the cause (the failed supply line to the laundry room).
4. Make a list of damaged items and their approximate value, and keep any damaged items until the adjuster has seen them.
5. Take reasonable steps to limit further damage, such as drying the area, running fans or dehumidifiers, and keeping receipts for any supplies or services.

On your question about a restoration company: please check with your insurer before hiring one, since some policies require the insurer's approval. Many insurers can also recommend a preferred vendor. If the area is at risk of mold or further damage, it's reasonable to start drying out now and document everything first.
